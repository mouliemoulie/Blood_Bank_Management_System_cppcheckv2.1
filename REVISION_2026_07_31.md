# BMS V2 Revision - 31 Jul 2026

Implemented changes include:
- Persistence load/save restored for hospital, inventory, donation, blood request, notification and emergency-alert modules.
- Donation camp module with persistent `donation_camps.dat` storage.
- Donation camp creation for Blood Bank Staff and viewing for Donors.
- Automatic donation-camp and emergency-alert notifications; menus use View Notifications instead of Process Notifications.
- Blood-request ordering uses priority, then Required By Date, then request ID/FIFO tie-break.
- Required By Date added to blood requests.
- Emergency alerts are independent of normal requests; source hospital and creator are linked from logged-in Hospital Staff.
- Emergency alerts are visible to Blood Bank Staff and Administrator.
- Hospital route entry moved to Hospital Staff and source hospital is auto-linked.
- Record Donation removed from Administrator/Hospital Staff menus and retained for Blood Bank Staff.
- Donor search supports ID, name, phone and blood group.
- Phone validation standardized to exactly 10 numeric digits.
- Email validation strengthened and reused for donor/hospital/camp input.
- User-facing IDs are displayed with prefixes in revised views where applicable.
- Inventory fulfilment now checks total stock before deduction to avoid partial deduction on insufficient stock.

Build verification command used:
`gcc -std=c11 -Wall -Wextra -Wpedantic -Wconversion -Wshadow *.c -o bms`

The container used for this revision did not provide cppcheck or Valgrind executables, so those two external-tool checks were not claimed as executed. GCC warning-enabled compilation was executed successfully.
